# 02 Game setup (Los geht's - eine Partie starten)

Handbook chapter 3, H p.5-6; readme chapter 3, R p.5.

## Setup sequence (first start)
1. Number of players: up to six (hotseat). H p.5
2. Mission difficulty: no missions, or easy / medium / hard. H p.5
3. Per player, in turn: name, sex (Geschlecht), faith (Glaube). Faith at start is Catholic or Protestant (evangelisch/lutherisch) only; leaving the church is possible later. H p.5
4. Colour (Farbe): used for the player's flags at branches and for their mercenaries/robbers in combat. H p.6
5. Hotkey: one keyboard key per player. In auctions pressing it places a bid; in a raid it lets the player change the tactics of each unit under their command. H p.6. Readme: chosen after entering the name. R p.7
6. The player's uncle bequeaths a branch in one of the "less significant" cities of Mittelland. H p.6
7. Player confirms the inputs. H p.6
8. If playing with missions: player draws a mission card of the colour matching the difficulty and enters any one of the three secret numbers printed on it; the game then knows the mission and detects its completion later. H p.6, R p.5

## Starting cities
- Six cities are handed out at game start. Only the first four of the six are navigable; Moortal and Falkenhorst cannot trade by ship. H p.6
- Each city's branch rights go to only one human player. H p.10

## Starting assets
- Goods in stock: none. H p.11
- Storage: 15 m². H p.15
- Trade: four of the six goods at the branch are darkened (not yet producible). H p.11
- Gold and transports: the two narrative sections disagree (see open question 1).
  - Introduction: a purse of 750 gold pieces and one cart. H p.38
  - Career chapter: "a warehouse and 500 gold pieces". H p.47
- Starting year 1600 (the uncle's last entries date from 1599). H p.38, p.56
- Player age: handbook says "assume you start in 1600 at age twenty". H p.56

## Missions (Aufträge)
Without missions there is no game goal; play is open-ended across generations. H p.5

| Difficulty | Card colour | Examples given | Duration hint |
|---|---|---|---|
| Easy (LEICHT) | green | become mayor of a city; reach a certain title | 1-2 h per player; 3-player game fits one evening |
| Medium (MITTEL) | blue | own a palace (Schloss) with extensive estates, park and outbuildings;
 criminal career breaking certain laws | longer |
| Hard (SCHWER) | red | become grand chancellor; own all three toll castles | longest |

H p.5-6, R p.5. Cards ship as PDFs `A1 - Aufträge - alle.pdf` (coloured), `A2`-`A4` (transparent, one per difficulty). Each card states its difficulty and carries three secret codes. R p.5
