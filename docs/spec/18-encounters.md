# 18 Encounters with wondrous figures (Begegnung mit wundersamen Gestalten)

Handbook H p.64.

## Random events named
Attending feasts; organising guild exhibitions (Zunftausstellungen); tax refunds; generous donations to fellow players. H p.64

## Adventures (sub-plots) named
A book offered for publishing; seminars on various topics; a famous scientist touring and lecturing; expeditions to distant lands; excavations under the residence's foundations; bets; competitions; helping solve a murder case. "Many other" events exist. H p.64

Properties: H p.64
- Usually span many game years.
- Participation recommended when affordable.
- Rewards: special skills, favour of several people, outwitting competitors, improving the purse.

See also 05-events.md (adventures from H p.29: peasants' revolt for toll castle owners; commission to topple an office holder).
