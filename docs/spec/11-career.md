# 11 An honourable career (Eine ehrbare Karriere)

Handbook H p.47-48: in-world application letter of "Bürger Heinrich". Narrative; rules below are what the text states or illustrates. Narrative numbers are examples, not engine constants.

## Stated rules
- The game starts with a warehouse in one of the less significant cities and 500 gold pieces (conflicts with 750 on H p.38; see 02-game-setup.md). H p.47
- Citizenship is granted after one year inside the city walls (the mayor "raised me to the citizen class"). H p.47; consistent with H p.28 (Bürger in 1601).
- Path to office: wait for a vacancy (e.g. an alderman died), apply, win the votes of the appointers. Aldermen are elected by treasurer, judge and master builder; winning two of three suffices in the example ("against the vote of the master builder"). H p.47, p.35
- Merits Heinrich cites in his letter (narrative; the text does not confirm them as mechanics): church donations ("reichliche Spenden"), friendship with the judge, feasts at his house, a park on his residence, a donated fountain, an upcoming marriage to an alderman's daughter, rescuing her from robbers. H p.47-48
- Heinrich was denounced by a rival to several office holders (narrative, H p.48); the denounce rule itself is H p.27.
- Bribery is a crime; it could have been used against Heinrich in court only if a competitor had had him watched (spied). H p.48
