# 16 Castles, rapiers, wild feasts (Schlösser, Degen, wilde Feste)

Handbook H p.60-61: in-world scene at Graf Baldur von Dekadent's feast. Narrative; rules below are those the text states or illustrates.

## Narrative content (illustrations from the scene; not confirmed as mechanics unless cross-referenced)
- Rich players have access to the pleasures of nobility (feasts etc.). H p.60
- Feasts: the host gives feasts in a hall with office holders as guests (archdeacon, priest, landgravine, abbot). H p.60-61
- Gambling ("17 und 4", dice "Schelmenbeine") with NPCs exists in narrative; winning at dice also appears as a fate event (H p.28). H p.60
- A toll castle can be bought from its owner by private deal (Waldenfels for 12,000 gold in the story). H p.61
- Duel: the insulted party chooses the weapon (rapier); outcome: standing back to "normal", health damaged. H p.61, p.34

- Mistress or lover: common; raises standing with like-minded people. H p.61
