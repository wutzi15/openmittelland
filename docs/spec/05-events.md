# 05 End of round, events and world turn

Handbook chapters 6 (Das Schicksal meint es gut... - Ereignisse, H p.28-29) and 7 (Geschehnisse im Mittelland, H p.29-30).

## 6.1 End of a player's round
Trigger: right-click in the office. If the warning option ("Runde bestätigen") is on, the player is asked to confirm; otherwise the round ends with the click. H p.28

Sequence after round end, in handbook order:
1. **Title award**: if the player has increased their gold diligently, a city's mayor may grant a new title. The first title, citizen (Bürger), is always granted in 1601, after one year living in a city; from then on the player may apply for offices. H p.28
2. **Cost report**: financial consequences of the year: workers' wages, transport costs; if goods were sold this round, this year's tax and church tithe; spies, mercenaries, robbers. H p.28
3. **Fate (Der liebe Zufall)**: random events after the cost report, positive or negative. H p.28
4. **Consequences of own actions (Selbst ist der Regenmacher)**: sabotage, raids, trials started by the player run as extra sequences. H p.28
5. **Adventures (Abenteuer)**: sub-plots spanning several years. H p.29
6. **Jury court**: if finances are out of control, goods in a branch or a cart may be seized; overstretching creditors leads to the jury court; bribery possible; debt tower threatens. H p.29

(Order of items 4-6 within the end-of-round flow is not explicitly stated beyond item 3 following item 2.)

### Fate examples (random events)
Stated examples: crop failure (Missernten); carters embezzle goods on transports; a country house is charged a land tax (Grundsteuer); workers, convinced of the player's poor situation, help out; winning gold at dice. H p.28
Further examples (H p.64): attending feasts, organising guild exhibitions, tax refunds, generous donations to fellow players.
Disasters named elsewhere: city disaster and siege shown in city info (H p.10); fire (storage, H p.15; in a city, R p.8); storm (residence renovation, H p.15); flood (Lauenburg 1616, narrative, H p.58).

### Adventures (sub-plots)
Multi-year "games within the game", usually a chance to improve wealth and standing. Stated examples: H p.29, p.64
- toll castle owner asked to help crush a peasants' revolt;
- antique shards found under the residence's foundations -> excavation;
- commission to bring about a specific person's fall from office;
- a book offered for publishing;
- seminars on various topics;
- a famous scientist touring and giving lectures;
- expeditions to distant lands;
- bets, competitions;
- solving a murder case.
Adventures may teach special skills, win favour, or improve the purse. H p.64
Toggled by option "Abenteuer". H p.8

### Jury court / debt
- Seizure (Pfändung) of stored goods or a cart ("Kuckuckssiegel"). H p.29
- Detailed rules in 13-law-books.md (debt, forced loans, seizure escalation).

## 7.1 Offices (world turn)
After all players end their round, computer characters act: some die, some are dismissed for incompetence, others move up into vacated offices. H p.29

Election screen: if a vacant office is one the player applied for, all candidates stand before the appointers on a special screen; a vote follows. Sometimes the player is an appointer and chooses a candidate (possibly deciding about another human player). H p.29

Background indicates office level: H p.29-30
| Background | Office type |
|---|---|
| Town hall portal (Rathausportal) | secular city office |
| Castle (Burg) | secular county office |
| Princely hall (fürstlicher Saal) | one of the highest secular offices |
| Church portal (Kirchenportal) | ecclesiastical city office |
| Cathedral (Dom) | ecclesiastical county office |
| Cathedral interior (Kathedraleninneres) | archbishop, archdeacon or inquisitor |

## 7.2 Other world events
Computer characters act freely: take fencing lessons, give feasts, change law books (if they have the privilege), change tax levels, besiege cities, hold competitions, swap minister chairs. Each pursues own goals; the player's opponent ("Gegenspieler") tries to obstruct the player. H p.30
Events affecting the player: transports raided by robbers; being taken to court; being insulted. H p.30

Insult: either let it rest (standing damaged) or demand satisfaction -> duel at dawn at the old bell tower; outcome depends on fencing skill. H p.30 (see 07-special-events.md)

Finally, if enabled, historical events of the year are reported. H p.30
