# 17 Banditry (Zum finst'ren Räuberthume)

Handbook H p.62-63: in-world memoir of Baron Hugo (1632). Narrative; rules below are those the text states or illustrates.

## Narrative content (illustrations from the memoir; not confirmed as mechanics unless cross-referenced)
- Unescorted transports are attractive raid targets. H p.62
- New noble titles are awarded by the regent ("Verleihung eines neuen Adelstitels durch den Regenten"). H p.62
- Goods that do not fit in storage are given away to the needy of the city (consistent with H p.15). H p.62

- A camp owner commands his camp through a named robber captain (Orlog). H p.62
- A camp owner can hire another camp owner's robbers for a fee (Baroness Natascha, 12,700 gold). H p.62
- Patrolling mercenaries ("hartnäckige Söldner") interfere with raids. H p.62
- Allied robbers took half of all captured goods despite providing only 12 robbers. H p.63
- Raids can be lucrative; robbers do not accept everything. H p.63
- Protection: escort every transport; request a military escort from a toll castle that patrols the trade routes. H p.63
