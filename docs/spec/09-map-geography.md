# 09 Map and geography (Die Karte von Mittelland / Eine kleine Geographie)

Handbook "Wichtige Hinweise": Einleitung H p.38, map H p.39 (image, checked in PDF render), geography H p.40.

## Setting
Germany, 1600; the small principality of Mittelland; Thirty Years' War era. H p.38

## Political structure
- Principality of Mittelland, capital Fürstenbrück, in the centre on the shore of the Great Lake (Großer See). H p.40
- Three counties: Mooringen, Auenthal, Bergenhöh. H p.40
- Ten cities, three toll castles, four robber camps. H p.6, p.21, p.40

Robber camps (hidden in gorges or dark forests): Meucheltann, Dunkelforst, Säbeltal, Henkerswald. H p.40

## Navigability
- Not reachable by ship: Moortal, Finsterwalde, Falkenhorst, Waldstein, and the four robber camps. H p.14
- Therefore by ship reachable (by exclusion): Fürstenbrück, Lichterheide, Lauenburg, Kronsbach, Greifenheim, Bergstedt. H p.14
- Start-city remark: Moortal and Falkenhorst are the two non-navigable start cities. H p.6

## Map image (H p.39)
The map shows the ten city names, a river system flowing from the north-east past Fürstenbrück through the Great Lake region to the southern sea ("Maris Germanica"), mountains along the north, forests in the west and south-east. No coordinates or distances are given in the handbook. Castles and robber camps are not labelled on this map.
